Time Spend
---
facebook 扎克伯格 PPT

# 1. There's always time. Time is priorities.

我们期望每天只花很少的时间在工作上，拥有更充足的睡眠，和更有趣的生活。
但实际我们花了太多时间在工作上，还花了一些时间做些无聊的事情。

时间是任何事必要的材料，如果要达到期望，我们必须要管理好时间。


# 2. Days always fill up

Only plan for 4-5 hours of real work per day.
工作会膨胀，每天安排4-5小时的工作，剩下的时间也会占用掉。
怎么解决这种情况？

# 3. Work more when you're in the zone. Relax when you're not.

It's normal to have days where you just can't work and days where you'll work 12 hours straight.
每个人的黄金时间并不一样，认知你自己，你自己的节奏，做好精力的管理。

个人精力和项目进度矛盾的时候？

# 4. Respect your time and make it respected.

Your time is $1000/hour, and you need to act accourdingly

反思一下，你现在在做的事情，能否达到你工资的价值？

# 5. Stop multi-tasking. It merely kills your focus.

Multi-tasking like a big shot; Single-treading and home at 5pm.

Answer emails => Work on a big presentation => Call clients => Feel productive


# 6. Set up a work routine and stick to it. Your body will adapt.

形成一定工作的套路，坚持成为习惯，适应下来，效率更高。

# 7. We're always more focused and productive with limited time.

有限的时间要求下，人们会变得更专注和高效。
聚焦 =》 效率，需要遗漏了。

# 8. Work is the best way to get working. Start with short tasks to get the ball rolling.
最好的进入工作状态的方法就是立马开始。从小任务开始，逐渐转动起来。

# 9. Work iteratively. Expectations to do things perfectly are stifling.

Doing is better than perfect.

每天迭代地前进一点，而不是停留在完美的幻想。

# 10. More work hours doesn't mean more productivity. Use constraints as oppertunities.

# 11. Separate brainless and strategic tasks to become more productive.

Separate thinking and execution to execute faster and think better.

# 12. Organize meetings early during the day. Time leading up to an event is often wasted.

需要沟通的（可够同时）尽早沟通。

# 13. Group meetings and communication (email or phone) to create blocks of uninterrupted work.

A single meeting can blow a whole afternoon, by breaking it into two pieces each too small to do anything hard in.

# 14. Keep the same context throughout the day. Switching between projects/clients is unproductive.
是当地，屏蔽一些打扰

# 15. Work around procrastination. Procrastinate between intense sprints of work (Pomodoro).
工作间隔拖延，

# 16. Break the unreasonable down into little reasonable chunks. A big goal is only achieved when every little thing that you do everyday, gets you closer to that goal.

复杂的需要分解；大目标需要拆解；

# 17. No 2 tasks ever hold the same importance. Always prioritize. Be really careful with to-do lists...

# 19. Break tasks into hour increments. Long tasks are hard to get into; feels like it all needs to get done.
任务应该分解到小时粒度？

# 20. Delegate and learn to make use of other people.
If something can be done 80% as well by someone else, delegate.

委托出去，授权，分而治之

# 21. Set deadlines for everything. Don't let tasks go on indefinitely.
任务无限地去做，工作无限膨胀

# 22. Set end dates for intense or stressful activities. Everything ends at some point.

# 23. Always take notes.
Get a reminder app for everything. Do not trust your own brain for your memory.

# 25. Write down anything that distracts you- google searches, random thoughts, new ideas, whatever. The point is, if you write them down, they'll stop bubbling up when you're in the zone.

# 27. Take break. Sometimes.
